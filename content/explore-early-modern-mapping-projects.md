**Early Modern Mapping Projects – Reference List**

**Visualizations**

  * [Death by Numbers ](https://deathbynumbers.org/visualizations/)
    * DataScribe, Tropy, Omeka, data-driven historical research, GIS, data visualizations, London 

**GIS**

  * [Decima ](https://decima-map.net/the-project/)
    * GIS, census data, population data, Florence, georectified historic map
  * [La Sfera ](https://lasfera.rrchnm.org/)
    * Manuscripts, Maps, trade routes, Italy, Mediterranean world, navigation, interactive map
  * [Mapping Mandeville Project ](https://historiacartarum.org/john-mandeville-and-the-hereford-map-2/)
    * Medieval, mappaemundi, the Hereford map, Sir John Mandeville, interactive image of digitized map (cities, people, monsters, legends, etc), annotated image.
  * [Books of Duchesses: Mapping Women Book Owners 1350-1550 ](https://booksofduchesses.com/)
    * Maps data related to medieval laywomen and their books, interactive map of Europe, network analysis/visualization
  * [The Atlas of Early Printing](https://atlas.lib.uiowa.edu/)
    * Interactive map, early history of printing in Europe, 1450-1500
  * [Mapping the Medieval Woman ](https://mappingthemedievalwoman.com/)
    * Interactive map, annotated image, medieval France, urban history
  * [The Berwickshire Place-Name Resource](https://berwickshire-placenames.glasgow.ac.uk/links/)
    * Scotland, place-names, interactive map, modern or historical base map, 1000-1900
  * [The Map of Early Modern London (MoEML) ](https://mapoflondon.uvic.ca/index.htm)
    * GUI – Graphical user interface, early modern London

**StoryMap**

  * [Incunabula: The Art & History of Printing in Western Europe, c. 1450-1500](https://www.loc.gov/ghe/cascade/index.html?appid=580edae150234258a49a3eeb58d9121c)
    * ArcGIS StoryMap, map visualization, interactive map

**Network Analysis**

  * [Early Modern Digital Itineraries (EMDigIt)](https://emdigit.org/2023/about-emdigit1/)
    * Network analysis, travel itineraries, palladio, python, transkribus
  * [Mapping the Republic of Letters](http://republicofletters.stanford.edu/)
    * Network analysis, visualizations

**Publications/Datasets**

  * [Historical Atlas of the Low Countries (1350-1800) ](https://datasets.iisg.amsterdam/dataset.xhtml?persistentId=hdl:10622/PGFYTM)
    * Dataset, geopkg files for download, the Netherlands, spatial features
  * [Visualising Urban Change. Bruges (Belgium), 1300-1700 ](https://researchportal.vub.be/en/publications/visualising-urban-change-bruges-belgium-1300-1700/)
    * PDF with visualizations, 1300-1700, GIS, Belgium
